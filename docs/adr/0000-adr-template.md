# ADR-NNNN: Short title of the decision

## Status

One of `Proposed`, `Accepted`, `Superseded by ADR-XXXX`, or `Deprecated`,
followed by the date (`YYYY-MM-DD`). Keep this line short; the history of the
decision lives in the sections below.

## Context

Describe the forces at play: the requirement or problem, the constraints
(technical, organisational, governance), and anything about the current state
of the system that makes a decision necessary now. State facts, not opinions.
Link to the concrete files or modules the decision concerns.

## Decision

State the decision in the active voice: "We will ...". Be specific and cite the
real code, configuration, or paths that implement it so the record stays
verifiable. One decision per ADR — if you are tempted to write "and also", that
is probably a second ADR.

## Alternatives Considered

List the serious options that were rejected. For each, note what it would have
bought and why it was not chosen. This is the most valuable section for a future
reader deciding whether to revisit the decision.

## Consequences

Describe the results of the decision — positive, negative, and neutral. Include
the new constraints the team now lives under and the follow-on work the decision
creates. Be honest about the trade-offs accepted.

## Future Considerations

Record the known follow-ups this decision leaves open: extensions that are
anticipated but not yet built, conditions that would trigger a re-evaluation,
and any debt deliberately deferred.
